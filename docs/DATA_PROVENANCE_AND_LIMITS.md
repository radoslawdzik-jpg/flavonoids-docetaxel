# Data provenance, boundaries and publication checklist

- DU-145 and HPrEC `data/processed/*biological_means.csv` were recovered from the earlier prepared reproducibility package. Each row is an experiment-level normalized response, representing four averaged technical replicates; original plate-level values are not included.
- `data/processed/manuscript_derived/` contains pre-existing CSVs prepared during manuscript development (S1, S2, S3, S5, S6, S7, S8, S9); filenames reflect original supplied naming, not a newly validated complete supplement. S4/S10/S11 are not included because no corresponding confirmed processed files were identified. Some files may reflect different development stages. Do not represent these as a finalized, independently audited supplement.
- `data/processed/transcriptomics/` holds previously computed exploratory GSE83654 gene-level and rank-based pathway data. These derive from public GEO source and the corresponding gene-set analyses; original GEO series matrix, GPL platform table, MSigDB resources and CTD export are deliberately excluded.
- `src/overlap.py` is a generic CTD/drug–gene overlap extension; it requires a separately supplied provenance-rich association CSV and does not recreate the paper's exact 22-gene CTD overlap without that input. No validated CTD association source was available for bundling.
- The GEO dye-swaps are technical measurements, not independent biological replicates. Gene signals are descriptive; do not claim validated log2 fold changes, differential expression significance or mechanistic validation of drug combinations.
- Prior reproducibility audit records a small unresolved discrepancy: Hill–Bliss holdout MAE rounds to 5.53 in the pipeline versus 5.54 in the manuscript. Other numerical outputs should be checked against the final article.
- Confirm that the authors have the right to redistribute the normalized experiment-level responses and derived tables. Check any third-party terms and attribution. No license to third-party content is granted by this archive.
- Before publication, verify the final manuscript's source data provenance, matching supplementary files, metadata, and any published code availability statement.
